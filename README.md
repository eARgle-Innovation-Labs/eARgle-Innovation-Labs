# eARgle Innovation Labs

### Technology Empowering Ability

We build smart assistive devices for people with visual, hearing and speech impairments, and we teach the embedded and IoT skills behind them.

eARgle Innovation Labs is the R&D and education brand of **eARgle Technologies LLP**, a DPIIT-recognised startup based in Vellore, Tamil Nadu, India.

---

## What we do

**Assistive technology.** Devices designed around real daily tasks for people with visual, hearing and speech impairments: hardware, firmware, mobile apps, and the cloud layer that ties them together.

**Product engineering.** Embedded and IoT product development for clients, covering ESP32 and ESP8266 firmware, sensor integration, Flutter apps, Firebase and Supabase backends, and deployment.

**IoT education.** Self-paced and live courses plus hands-on workshops for schools and colleges, delivered through [earglelabs.com](https://earglelabs.com).
